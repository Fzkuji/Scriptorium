# Calvin's move to Japan and his mansion

Calvin, a musician, made a big life change in early 2023: he moved into a new
mansion in Japan and set out to immerse himself in Japanese culture.

## The move and the house

On [2023-03-23](../../timeline/2023/03-Mar.md) Calvin announced a big life
change and showed off his new mansion, a building with a sign on the front
[D1:3]. He was excited to learn about Japanese culture and to expand [D1:5], and
though he had never been to Japan before, he was fascinated by its traditions
[D1:7]. He planned to leave the following month (April 2023) [D1:9] and to stay
a few months in Japan before heading to Boston [D1:15]. His agent found him the
place to stay, a cozy living room with a couch, table, and television [D1:9,
D1:11].

The mansion sits amid a stunning cityscape. On
[2023-07-07](../../timeline/2023/07-Jul.md) Calvin shared a sunset view from his
backyard, a boat docked in a canal at sunset, comparing living there to a dream
come true [D10:6, D10:8]. The house has both a music studio and a separate
relaxation room with a TV where he unwinds and gets inspired
([2023-11-02](../../timeline/2023/11-Nov.md)) [D28:29].

## The flood

Calvin's place got flooded in the week before
[2023-05-16](../../timeline/2023/05-May.md). He managed to save his music gear
and his favorite microphone, and was waiting on insurance before starting
repairs; he shared a photo of his studio with a keyboard and synthesizer
[D6:3, D6:5].

## Turning the mansion into a studio

By [2023-07-21](../../timeline/2023/07-Jul.md) Calvin was transforming the
Japanese mansion into a recording studio, a longtime dream of having his own
space to create music with other artists; he shared a photo of the in-progress
room with a ladder [D11:4]. It was in this home studio that he later recorded
with Frank Ocean (see music-career.md).

## Japan travel plans

On [2023-06-21](../../timeline/2023/06-Jun.md) Calvin shared a view of a small
Japanese town with mountains seen from his living room, a place he had not yet
visited but planned to see after the Frank Ocean tour [D9:11, D9:13, D9:15]. He
reflected on how incredible his Japan trip had been on
[2023-09-22](../../timeline/2023/09-Sep.md), already longing to go back [D20:4].
After the tour ended he planned to return to Tokyo in November 2023
([2023-10-19](../../timeline/2023/10-Oct.md)), where he was excited to visit
Shibuya Crossing and Shinjuku and to finally try the food, including ramen,
which he had never tried [D24:17, D24:19, D24:21]. On
[2023-11-17](../../timeline/2023/11-Nov.md) he shared a photo he shot in a
Japanese garden, a bench under a tree with pink flowers [D30:16].
