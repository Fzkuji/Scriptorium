# Calvin's music career

Calvin is a rap/music artist whose 2023 arc runs from studio sessions and a
Tokyo festival, through a tour with Frank Ocean, to releasing his own album and
mentoring the next generation.

Calvin met Frank Ocean at a festival in August the previous year (August 2022)

[D24:5]. He had an early taste of the tour by
[2023-05-31](../../timeline/2023/05-May.md), when touring with Frank Ocean the

[2023-08-22](../../timeline/2023/08-Aug.md) he landed a deal to continue

[2023-10-19](../../timeline/2023/10-Oct.md), with amazing crowd energy [D24:1,
D24:3]. By [2023-10-23](../../timeline/2023/10-Oct.md) Calvin admitted fame made
