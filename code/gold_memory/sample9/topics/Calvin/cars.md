# Calvin's cars

Calvin is proud of his luxury cars, which he treats as symbols of his hard work.
Over 2023 he buys a red sports car, has a minor accident, and later gets a
Ferrari.

## The red sports car

On [2023-03-26](../../timeline/2023/03-Mar.md) Calvin shared the news that he had
gotten a new car, fulfilling his dream of owning a luxury car [D2:1]. It is a red
sports car, which he photographed on 2023-03-25 (the day before) and drove like
a rockstar [D2:5]. He put a lot of work into it and displayed it at a show
([2023-04-20](../../timeline/2023/04-Apr.md)) [D3:13]. Long drives in it help him
relax, with the wind and the open road ([2023-05-03](../../timeline/2023/05-May.md))
[D5:8]. He treats the car as a symbol of how far he has come
([2023-09-22](../../timeline/2023/09-Sep.md)) [D20:2].

## The car accident

Calvin had a minor car accident on the Friday before
[2023-06-21](../../timeline/2023/06-Jun.md) (**2023-06-16**); no one was hurt,
and he shared a photo of a red car with a black rim on a sidewalk [D9:1]. The
insurance process was slow and full of paperwork but got sorted; a tow truck and
a skilled mechanic in a garage handled it [D9:3, D9:5, D9:7]. Sorting everything
out took a week and the cost was not too bad [D9:5]. By
[2023-07-07](../../timeline/2023/07-Jul.md) the car was fully fixed and running
strong [D10:2].

## The Ferrari

Calvin owns a Ferrari he is deeply attached to. On
[2023-08-03](../../timeline/2023/08-Aug.md) he took his Ferrari for a service and
found it stressful [D12:1], and recalled a recent trip to a Ferrari dealership
where he saw many amazing cars (image: a red car lifted in a garage) [D12:7]. He
got another new Ferrari the week before
[2023-10-15](../../timeline/2023/10-Oct.md), calling it a masterpiece on wheels
(image: a black sports car in front of a building) [D23:16].
