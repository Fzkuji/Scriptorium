# Dave's background and how he got into cars

Dave's love of cars traces back to childhood with his father, and it grew into a
career in automotive engineering.

## Growing up with his dad

Dave grew up working on cars with his dad, which gives him a lasting sense of
fulfillment ([2023-08-03](../../timeline/2023/08-Aug.md)) [D12:2]. He and his dad
spent **one summer restoring an old car together** — hard work, but deeply
satisfying to finish together (image: a man and a child posing) [D12:4, D12:6].
As a child he spent hours in his dad's garage tinkering with engines, his own
sanctuary ([2023-10-08](../../timeline/2023/10-Oct.md)) [D22:5].

## Discovering car engineering

Ever since he was **ten**, Dave has been fascinated by how machines work. He
found an old car in a neighbor's garage and asked to fix it, and transforming it
from broken-down to high-running hooked him
([2023-10-23](../../timeline/2023/10-Oct.md)) (image: a man working on a car)
[D25:15]. His dad took him to his **first car show at age 10**, which sparked his
love of cars; he is now an **automotive engineer**
([2023-10-25](../../timeline/2023/10-Oct.md)) [D26:6].
