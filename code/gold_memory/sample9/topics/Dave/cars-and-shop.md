# Dave's cars and car shop

Dave is a car enthusiast and automotive engineer in Boston. Over 2023 he opens
his own maintenance shop, joins workshops, and restores a string of classic
cars.

## Passion for classic cars

Dave attended a classic-car show around
[2023-03-23](../../timeline/2023/03-Mar.md) and spoke with owners about their
cars [D1:2]. He went to another car show the weekend before
[2023-04-20](../../timeline/2023/04-Apr.md), sharing a green Mustang photo and
explaining his passion for auto engineering [D3:12]. His long-term dream is to
work on classic cars for their design and engineering
([2023-05-01](../../timeline/2023/05-May.md)) [D4:5], and he had already restored
a classic car the previous year (2022), sharing a photo of its engine [D4:7].

## Opening his own shop

Dave opened his own **car maintenance shop** on
[2023-05-01](../../timeline/2023/05-May.md), a dream come true (image: a car
dealership with cars parked in front); at the shop he does everything from
regular maintenance to full restorations of classic cars [D4:1, D4:19]. He
celebrated with friends the week before
[2023-05-16](../../timeline/2023/05-May.md) [D6:8]. He also started teaming up
with a **local garage** on [2023-05-03](../../timeline/2023/05-May.md), working
alongside other mechanics, with a dream of one day building a custom car from
scratch [D5:1, D5:3, D5:5]. He fixed his neighbor's car engine himself by
[2023-05-31](../../timeline/2023/05-May.md) [D7:2, D7:4].

## Workshops and skill-building

Dave was picked for a **car-modification workshop**
([2023-08-11](../../timeline/2023/08-Aug.md)), a dream come true for someone who
always wanted to learn auto engineering and build a custom car [D13:1, D13:3]. He
had been doing engine swaps and suspension mods, and was learning body
modifications on a classic muscle car (image: a silver Corvette) [D13:7]. He
went to a **car-restoration workshop in San Francisco** on
[2023-08-14](../../timeline/2023/08-Aug.md) [D14:1], and returned from San
Francisco with new car-modification insights on
[2023-09-02](../../timeline/2023/09-Sep.md) [D17:1]. He attended a **conference
in Detroit** and learned a lot ([2023-10-25](../../timeline/2023/10-Oct.md))
[D26:2].

## Setbacks and the blog

A competing car maintenance shop won a deal Dave had chased for months, leaving
him discouraged ([2023-10-15](../../timeline/2023/10-Oct.md)) [D23:1]. Dave
invited Calvin to visit his garage when Calvin is in Boston (image: a car in a

[D18:10, D18:12]. He started a **blog about car mods** to share his passion
([2023-11-02](../../timeline/2023/11-Nov.md)) [D28:8], including a post about
making a car look like a beast that inspired others to start DIY projects (image:
a blue Subaru) [D28:10].
