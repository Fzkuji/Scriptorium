[2023-05-07](timeline/2023/05-May.md) and found it powerful [D1:3]. The
transgender stories she heard there inspired her [D1:5], and the group made
her feel accepted, giving her courage to embrace herself [D1:7]. She told

who supported her through negative experiences [D2:10, D3:11]. Melanie has
