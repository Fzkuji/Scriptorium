## Adoption journey

Caroline decided to pursue adopting children in need, seeing it as her way
of building a family [D2:9]. By [2023-05-25](timeline/2023/05-May.md) she was
researching adoption agencies [D2:8], and she chose a specific agency for its
LGBTQ-friendly values and inclusive process [D2:11]. When Melanie asked what
made her pick that agency, Caroline said the staff made her feel completely
accepted [D2:11, D2:12].

## Motivation

Her decision is rooted in her own experience of feeling supported by chosen
family: the LGBTQ support group she attended on
[2023-05-07](timeline/2023/05-May.md) gave her "courage to embrace herself"
[D1:7], and she wants to give kids the same sense of belonging [D2:9].
